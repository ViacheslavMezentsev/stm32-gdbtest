# rc3 R13: WFI, wakeup and delay progress

[Documentation](index.md) · [Русский](../ru/r13.md)

2026-10-02, after `869f884`, branch codex/rc3-api-r1. Core, public API,

## Method and results

[Two scenarios](../../../../tests/api-experiments/profile/tests/board/test_wfi_path.py)
exercise ordinary Sleep on BlackPill F411CE/ST-Link/OpenOCD0.12.0 HLA/SWD,
Windows, GDB14/16. The delay_ms == 500 condition separates the intended board_delay_ms
from the short ADC initialization delay. Within function bounds obtained through
gdb.Block, disassembly finds one WFI; its bytes are checked against 30bf.

The scenario stops before WFI (0x0800025e), then at the exact selected IRQ entry.
Skipping SIGTRAMP_FRAME, it looks for interrupted board_delay_ms with the PC
immediately after WFI (0x08000260). Attempts are bounded at eight. Following natural
return, it checks that PC, restored SP, Thread mode and handler work.

| Scenario | Isolation | Verified behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SYSTICK | Enabled external IRQs disabled | Exception15, ticks advanced |
| TIM2 | TIM2 retained, SysTick stopped and its pending cleared | Exception44, timer_events advanced, ticks unchanged |

The finally block restores NVIC enable masks and SysTick control bits. Subsequent
execution reaches app_loop, ticks advance by at least500 and ADC sequences remain1.
This also checks that the delay works again after restoration.

**16/16 HW PASS:** two scenarios × four runs × two GDBs. No unexpected FAIL/ERROR.
Baseline and final HAL restore: HW_BOOT/HW_GPIO PASS. No remaining GDB/OpenOCD
processes. Logs confirm hardware Z1 without Z0; all four fault guards retained.

## Techniques and limits

- IRQ entry alone is insufficient: TIM2 included a stop with the interrupted PC
  still at WFI. Checking the function and post-instruction PC identifies the
  intended path; bounded retries handle this observation without indefinite waiting.
- CPU wakeup and application time progress are distinct events. TIM2 executes,
  but the ticks-based delay cannot complete while SysTick is stopped.
- Settings are restored, but pending bits, COUNTFLAG, timer phase and counters
  are not rolled back. Reading SysTick CTRL affects COUNTFLAG; clearing pending
  SysTick in TIM2 is an intentional intervention. Complete firmware restoration
  is performed separately after the matrix.
- This demonstrates a control path with debugger stops. A pending IRQ can make
  WFI complete immediately; sleep residency, power and latency were not measured.
  STOP/STANDBY, WFE, SLEEPONEXIT, PSP/RTOS and other boards were not tested.
  Earlier native DAP/stepi limitations from R4 remain.
